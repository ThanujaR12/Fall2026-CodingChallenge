# Quickstart: verifying Palette Boards & Standout Extras

Run the app as in `README.txt`, sign up, then walk through these. Each maps to a user story.

1. **Palette (US1, US4)**: On Home, Save three photos to a new board. Open the board: five swatches,
   hover shows a hex code, click copies it; Copy all and Download work. Make an empty board: grey
   placeholder with "Save photos to see this board's colors."
2. **Theme (US2)**: The board's header is washed in its colours with a palette stripe; text is readable.
3. **Colour search (US3)**: Search "flowers", pick Pink → pink photos. Palettes → Black and white →
   no colour photos. On a board, "Find more like this palette" → photos in its main colour; Save one.
4. **Explore (US5)**: Share → turn on "Public on Explore". In a private window (signed out) open
   `/explore`: the board appears; filter by its colour; open it read-only and themed.
5. **Instant UI (US6)**: Save a photo → the button reads "Saved" immediately. Edit a title → shows at
   once. (To see a rollback, go offline in DevTools and edit again: the form reopens with your text.)
6. **Notifications (US7)**: Invite a second account as editor; as that account add a photo; back as
   the owner, the bell shows a count; the panel lists the invite and the addition; Mark all as read.
7. **Activity (US8)**: The board page's "Recent activity" lists who added, edited, or removed what.
8. **Additions (US9)**: Home feed and topic chips; the sidebar peeks then hides and returns at the
   left edge; Create page with preview; microphone in the search bar (Chrome/Edge/Safari).
